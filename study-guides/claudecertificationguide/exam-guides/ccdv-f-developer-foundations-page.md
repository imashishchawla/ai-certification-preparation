Who the exam is for

Anthropic aims this exam at AI engineers and software developers with one to five years of engineering experience and at least six months of hands-on work with Claude or comparable LLM systems, proficient in Python and/or TypeScript and fluent with REST APIs and CLI tools. Recommended, not required — the credential is awarded on exam performance alone.
