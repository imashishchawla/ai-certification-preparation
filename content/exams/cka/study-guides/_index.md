## 2. Domain Deep Dives (D1–D5)

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="question-card" style="border-left: 6px solid var(--accent);">
    <span class="tag">DOMAIN 1 · 30%</span>
    <h3><a href="/ai-certification-preparation/exams/cka/domains/d1/">D1: Troubleshooting</a></h3>
    <p>Cluster, node, static pod, and CoreDNS diagnosis; metrics inspection with `kubectl top`; and container exit codes.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="question-card" style="border-left: 6px solid var(--accent);">
    <span class="tag">DOMAIN 2 · 25%</span>
    <h3><a href="/ai-certification-preparation/exams/cka/domains/d2/">D2: Cluster Architecture &amp; Installation</a></h3>
    <p>Bootstrapping clusters with `kubeadm`, executing version upgrades, securing APIs with RBAC, and `etcd` backup/restore.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="question-card" style="border-left: 6px solid var(--accent);">
    <span class="tag">DOMAIN 3 · 20%</span>
    <h3><a href="/ai-certification-preparation/exams/cka/domains/d3/">D3: Services &amp; Networking</a></h3>
    <p>Pod networking, Service types, Ingress routes, Gateway API resources, and NetworkPolicy ingress/egress rules.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="question-card" style="border-left: 6px solid var(--accent);">
    <span class="tag">DOMAIN 4 · 15%</span>
    <h3><a href="/ai-certification-preparation/exams/cka/domains/d4/">D4: Workloads &amp; Scheduling</a></h3>
    <p>Deployment lifecycles, ConfigMaps, Secrets, Horizontal Pod Autoscaling (HPA), affinity, taints, and tolerations.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="question-card" style="border-left: 6px solid var(--accent);">
    <span class="tag">DOMAIN 5 · 10%</span>
    <h3><a href="/ai-certification-preparation/exams/cka/domains/d5/">D5: Storage</a></h3>
    <p>StorageClasses, dynamic provisioning, access modes, Persistent Volumes (PVs), and Persistent Volume Claims (PVCs).</p>
  </div>
</div>
