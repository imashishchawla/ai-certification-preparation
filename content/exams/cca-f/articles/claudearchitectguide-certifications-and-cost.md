Anthropic certifications: all four Claude exams, what they cost, and who can take them

Anthropic runs 4 role-based Claude certifications, proctored through Pearson VUE and priced from $99 to $175 per attempt. All four run 120 minutes and pass at 720 on a 100–1,000 scale. The rule that catches most people is not on the price list: registration needs a work email on a Claude Partner Network domain, and personal addresses are rejected.

IN SHORT
There are four exams, not one. An Associate tier, a Developer tier, and two Architect tiers, Foundations and Professional.
They cost $99 to $175 per attempt. All four run 120 minutes and pass at 720 on a 100–1,000 scale.
You may not be able to register. Every exam needs a work email on a Claude Partner Network domain, and personal addresses are rejected.
They expire after 12 months. Renewing on time is a free assessment rather than a re-sit.
Anthropic's free courses are not these. Those award certificates of completion, and the difference is worth understanding before you spend anything.
The four exams

Anthropic runs four role-based Claude certifications, all proctored through Pearson VUE and all registered through the Anthropic Partner Academy. They divide by role rather than by seniority. The Associate exam is aimed at people who use Claude in their day-to-day work, the Developer exam at people who build on the Claude API, and the two Architect exams at people who design the systems those others work inside.

WHERE THE QUESTION COUNTS COME FROM

Anthropic publishes the price, duration, pass mark, validity and retake policy for all four exams in one place, and a question count for none of them. Three of the four publish one in their own exam guide instead, which is where the counts in this table come from, and where the 63 for CCAR-P has always come from. CCAO-F publishes no count anywhere primary. Several prep sites state one anyway without citing a source, so this table says "not published" rather than guessing.

The eligibility rule almost nobody leads with

Registration requires a partner email address on a recognised company domain. Anthropic's certification FAQ puts it plainly: personal email addresses will not work. Pearson VUE's programme page says the same thing from the other direction, describing certification as open to organizations in the Claude Partner Network and counting toward partner program standing.

A Gmail or Outlook address is rejected at the attestation step, before you reach payment. This is the most common reason somebody reads a study guide, plans a revision timetable and then discovers there is nothing they can book.

There is a way through, and it costs less than it sounds. The Claude Partner Network is free to join at its entry tier, so a two-person consultancy with its own domain can become eligible without paying for membership. What you cannot do is register as a private individual with no organisation behind you.

Why the credential exists, and why it expires

Certified headcount feeds Anthropic's partner tiering. Partner organisations need certified staff to reach and hold a tier, which is why the exams are gated to partner domains and why a credential lapses after twelve months rather than lasting indefinitely. A count that never expired would stop describing anything real.

That shapes what the certification is worth to you. As a signal inside the partner ecosystem it is concrete, recognised, and usually has a budget line attached. As a signal on the open job market it is young, and few hiring managers outside that ecosystem will recognise the acronym yet. Whether it is worth your time depends mostly on which of those two rooms you are standing in.

Retakes, renewal, and what failing actually costs

You may attempt an exam four times per rolling twelve months. The waits after a failure run 14 days, then 30, then 90. Renewal at the twelve-month mark is a free, non-proctored assessment on Anthropic Partner Academy.

The money is the smaller cost. A failed first attempt at the Professional exam is $175 and a fortnight; a failed third is $175 and a quarter of a year, which can push a renewal deadline out of reach entirely. Sitting a full-length mock before booking is worth more here than on a certification you could simply re-sit next week.

Which one should you take?

Match the exam to what you actually do rather than to how senior you are. Someone who has never designed a retrieval pipeline will struggle with the Architect exams regardless of years served, and an engineer who ships against the API every day may find the Developer credential the more useful one to hold. The full comparison works through the trade-offs exam by exam.

One limit of this page is worth stating outright: it describes the programme, not the syllabus. Each exam has its own blueprint with its own domain weights, and only the Professional Architect blueprint is covered in depth anywhere on this site.

Can anyone take an Anthropic certification exam?

No. Registration for all four Claude certification exams requires a work email address on a domain belonging to an organisation in the Claude Partner Network, and Anthropic's own certification FAQ states that personal email addresses will not work. Joining the Partner Network at its entry tier is free, so a small consultancy can become eligible without paying for membership, but someone with only a personal address cannot register at all.

How much does an Anthropic certification cost?

Between $99 and $175 per attempt, before partner discounts. The Claude Certified Associate exam is $99, the Foundations-level Developer and Architect exams are $125 each, and the Claude Certified Architect — Professional exam is $175. Anthropic's Academy courses are a separate programme and cost nothing.

How long does an Anthropic certification last?

Twelve months from the date you earn it. Renewal is a free, non-proctored assessment on Anthropic Partner Academy rather than a second sitting of the exam, and renewing on time costs nothing. The annual expiry exists because certified headcount feeds partner tiering, so the number has to describe something current.

How many times can you retake a Claude certification exam?

Four times per exam in any rolling twelve months. After a failed first attempt you wait 14 days, after a second 30 days, and after a third 90 days. At $99 to $175 an attempt, the waiting periods cost more than the money does.

Are Anthropic certification exams multiple choice?

Yes, in two forms. Anthropic's FAQ says all four exams use multiple choice and scenario-based multiple response questions, with 120 minutes to answer them. The multiple-response items decide most results, because they carry no partial credit: a set that is right except for one extra selection scores zero.
