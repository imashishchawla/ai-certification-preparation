Long-running work needs a way to preserve the facts that matter. This domain covers what belongs in the current context, what should be stored as durable state, and how a workflow can resume after interruption. Reliability also means making failures visible and setting clear limits on retries and tool use.

## What to practice

- Keep essential task state available without filling context with repeated material.
- Record progress in a form that a resumed workflow can use.
- Bound retries and verify outcomes before treating a task as complete.
