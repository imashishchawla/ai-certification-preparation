This domain focuses on choosing the right structure for a task. A fixed workflow is useful when the steps and checks are known. An agent loop is more suitable when the next action depends on observations. Multiple agents can divide independent work, but they also introduce coordination and handoff costs.

## What to practice

- Identify where a deterministic workflow should replace open-ended agent behavior.
- Decide what context and outputs a delegated task needs.
- Trace how a coordinator verifies results and handles a failed handoff.
